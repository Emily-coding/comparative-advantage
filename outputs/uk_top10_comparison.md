### Is the claim still true in 2024?

**Against the published 1989 list:**

- 8 of the 10 are in the 2024 top 10 (the report found 7 for 2019).
- Still in: Financial services, Beverages, Dyeing, tanning and colouring materials, Professional, scientific, controlling instruments, apparatus, nes, Medicinal and pharmaceutical products, Other transport equipment, Oils and perfume materials; toilet and cleansing preparations, Power generating machinery and equipment.
- Dropped out: Chemical materials and products, nes, Office machines and automatic data processing equipment.
- Top two in 1989: Beverages, Financial services. Top two in 2024: Power generating machinery and equipment, Financial services. Same pair: **no**.

**Against the recomputed 1989 list:**

- 7 of the 10 are in the 2024 top 10 (the report found 7 for 2019).
- Still in: Beverages, Medicinal and pharmaceutical products, Financial services, Power generating machinery and equipment, Dyeing, tanning and colouring materials, Oils and perfume materials; toilet and cleansing preparations, Professional, scientific, controlling instruments, apparatus, nes.
- Dropped out: Chemical materials and products, nes, Organic chemicals, Office machines and automatic data processing equipment.
- Top two in 1989: Beverages, Medicinal and pharmaceutical products. Top two in 2024: Power generating machinery and equipment, Financial services. Same pair: **no**.

### How close is the recomputed 2019 list to the published one?

- 8 of the 10 published 2019 products are in the recomputed 2019 top 10.
- Published but not recomputed: ICT, Transport.
- Recomputed but not published: Non-ferrous metals, Chemical materials and products, nes.
